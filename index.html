Frequently asked questions

Can I book a real flight on this website?

No. This is a portfolio demo. You can explore sample flights, but no reservation is made and no payment is taken.

Which routes can I explore?

The demo includes selected European routes. Available options will be shown on the flight search page.

How is the total price calculated?

The total is the selected flight’s fare per passenger multiplied by the number of passengers.
